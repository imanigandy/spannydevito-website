A diary

Spanny's Timeline

From puppy mill to forever home. It got complicated in the middle.

22 May 2026
Adoption Day

He comes home.

We bring Spanny home. He is suspicious of everything. We are delighted. He is built like a little ottoman with legs and he has the energy of someone who has been wronged and is biding his time.

27 May 2026
Emergency

3:00 AM.

Acute symptoms begin. Nobody sleeps.

28 May 2026
Emergency

Portia leaves. Spanny continues pooping water.

Portia leaves to visit her mom. Spanny continues pooping water. I rush him to the emergency vet. He is hospitalized for dehydration and observation. The first concern is parvo. It is ruled out.

Rocky Mountain Cocker Rescue is responsive and concerned. The bill is not yet $3,000.

29 May 2026
Emergency

AHDS.

Acute Hemorrhagic Diarrhea Syndrome diagnosis. IV fluids. Nobody is having a good time.

30 May 2026
Recovery

He comes home. The bill does not.

Spanny comes home. PCR panel still pending. The bill is nearly $3,000. I wince and put it on a credit card. I reach out to the rescue and ask them to cover half. They become noticeably less responsive and less concerned.

31 May 2026
Recovery

Portia comes home.

Portia comes home to find me exhausted and at my wit's end.

2 June 2026
Diagnosis

Giardia.

PCR results come back: giardia. The treating vet notes the infection was likely contracted prior to adoption or during transport. We began furiously deep cleaning the house. Every surface. Every dog bed. Every crate. I threaten to burn the house down approximately seventeen times. Portia remains calm. I do not understand this.

5 June 2026
Rescue

The call that wasn't.

Conference call scheduled with rescue leadership to discuss the bill. They reschedule. Then go silent.

11 June 2026
Rescue

They decline.

The rescue declines to help. They also decline to return our $450 adoption fee, which seems rude. Still waiting on a clean fecal test. Spanny remains unimpressed by all of it.

17 June 2026
Recovery

Checkup.

Vet appointment for a checkup and discussion about the giardia. Progress noted. Still monitoring.

19 June 2026
Milestone

Free at last.

Juneteenth. Ironically, Spanny is cleared of giardia. We are now integrating him into the pack. He has been through a lot. He remains suspicious of everything. We remain delighted.
